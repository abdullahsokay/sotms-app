What is SOTMS?

SOTMS is a fleet-intelligence platform built for oil and logistics operators. Track every vehicle in real time, monitor engine and vehicle health, and keep a close eye on fuel usage and driver behaviour — all from one dashboard, on your phone.

📍 Live GPS TrackingSee every vehicle's exact location and route in real time.
🛠️ Vehicle DiagnosticsMonitor engine health and get alerts before problems grow.
⛽ Fuel AnalyticsTrack consumption and spot losses or irregular usage.
👤 Driver InsightsUnderstand driver behaviour and keep your fleet safe.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file.
  3. Android will ask permission to install from this source — tap Settings, turn it on, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then open SOTMS and sign in.
Seeing a warning? Android shows a caution message for any app installed outside the Play Store. SOTMS is safe — the app is digitally signed by HackTech Zone. Tap Install anyway to continue.
